# Euler's Identity

## Equation

$$
\mark[exponential]{e}^{\mark[imaginary]{i}\mark[pi]{\pi}} + \mark[one]{1} = \mark[zero]{0}
$$

## Description

Starting at 1, [rotating]{.exponential} by [half a turn]{.pi} in the [complex plane]{.imaginary} lands at −1. Adding [1]{.one} brings us to [0]{.zero}.

## .exponential

Euler's number $e \approx 2.71828$. The base of continuous growth. With an imaginary exponent, $e^{i\theta}$ traces the unit circle.

## .imaginary

The imaginary unit $i = \sqrt{-1}$. Turns exponential growth sideways, converting it into rotation in the complex plane.

## .pi

The constant $\pi \approx 3.14159$. The angle of rotation in radians: 180°, a half-turn.

## .one

The number 1. Offsets the result of the rotation $e^{i\pi} = -1$, cancelling it out.

## .zero

Zero. The origin of the complex plane, where the rotation and offset balance perfectly.
