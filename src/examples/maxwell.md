# Maxwell's Equations

## Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mark[divergence]{\nabla \cdot} \mark[electric]{\vec{E}} &= \frac{\mark[charge]{\rho}}{\mark[permittivity]{\varepsilon_0}} \\
\mark[divergence]{\nabla \cdot} \mark[magnetic]{\vec{B}} &= \mark[zero]{0} \\
\mark[curl]{\nabla \times} \mark[electric]{\vec{E}} &= -\mark[timederiv]{\frac{\partial}{\partial t}}\mark[magnetic]{\vec{B}} \\
\mark[curl]{\nabla \times} \mark[magnetic]{\vec{B}} &= \mark[permeability]{\mu_0}\mark[current]{\vec{J}} + \mark[permeability]{\mu_0}\mark[permittivity]{\varepsilon_0}\mark[timederiv]{\frac{\partial}{\partial t}}\mark[electric]{\vec{E}}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Description

[Electric fields]{.electric} [flow out]{.divergence} from [positive charges]{.charge} and into negative ones; [magnetic fields]{.magnetic} have [no endpoints]{.zero}. [Changing]{.timederiv} fields create each other, the basis of light and all electromagnetic waves.

## .divergence

The divergence operator $\nabla \cdot$. Measures how much a field flows outward or inward at a point.

## .curl

The curl operator $\nabla \times$. Measures how much a field swirls or rotates around a point.

## .electric

The electric field $\vec{E}$. Force per unit charge. Pushes charges. It can spread out from charges or circulate when magnetic fields change.

## .magnetic

The magnetic field $\vec{B}$. Deflects moving charges. Its field lines have no beginnings or endings.

## .charge

Charge density $\rho$. Amount of electric charge per volume. The source of electric fields.

## .permittivity

Permittivity of free space $\varepsilon_0$. Sets the electric field’s response to charge.

## .zero

Zero divergence. No magnetic sources or sinks; field lines need not form closed loops.

## .timederiv

Time derivative $\frac{\partial}{\partial t}$. Rate of change. Changing fields induce each other, electromagnetic induction.

## .permeability

Permeability of free space $\mu_0$. Sets the magnetic field’s response to current. With $\varepsilon_0$, sets the speed of light.

## .current

Current density $\vec{J}$. Electric current per unit area. Creates magnetic fields that swirl around it.
